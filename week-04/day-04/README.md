# Week 4, day 4: case #123 explained, selection rates by group, and the technique chosen

Three exercises, in order. Each runs in the class's Databricks workspace: open your Git folder of the course, pull, and open the exercise's notebook from it. Each reads made-up files beside its notebook, not your week 3 tables. The course made up every file and every value; groups A and B describe no real person and stand for no protected class, and nothing describes how the IRS selects returns.

1. [Name the top three features, then explain case #123 in two sentences](exercise-01-explain-case-123/README.md). What you do: read your candidate's ranked chart, then case #123's chart, check that its pushes add up to its score, and write the two sentences an examiner reads. Where you work: the notebook `exercise-01-explain-case-123/exercise.ipynb`. Done when: cell 6 prints `Done:`. [Start](exercise-01-explain-case-123/) · [Answer](answers/exercise-01-explain-case-123/)
2. [Compare selection rates by group, then say whether a feature could stand in for the group](exercise-02-selection-rates-by-group/README.md). What you do: compute each group's selection rate as a division, state the gap with its counts, and compare one feature's typical value in the two groups. Where you work: the notebook `exercise-02-selection-rates-by-group/exercise.ipynb`. Done when: cell 6 prints `Done:`. [Start](exercise-02-selection-rates-by-group/) · [Answer](answers/exercise-02-selection-rates-by-group/)
3. [For two scenario tasks, choose a trained model or prompting, and give the reason](exercise-03-choose-the-technique/README.md). What you do: answer labelled data, cost and risk for two made-up tasks, choose the technique for each, and name the row that decided it. Where you work: the notebook `exercise-03-choose-the-technique/exercise.ipynb`, text only. Done when: cell 4 prints `Done:`.
